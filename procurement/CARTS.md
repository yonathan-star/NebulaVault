Both carts were created through real vendor shopping controls. No checkout, account creation, order, payment, wishlist publication or message was performed.

| Vendor | Final items | Merchandise | Shipping estimate | Tariff | Displayed total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| DigiKey | 10 line items; wires quantity two, cable ties quantity four, others one | $57.39 | $4.99 | $0.15 | $62.53 estimate |

All final priced cart lines showed stock available/immediate. DigiKey's backordered Perma-Proto was removed and replaced with the same Adafruit product in stock. The Adafruit resistor pack was out of stock and never added; a matching individual 470-ohm resistor was used instead.

Adafruit displays $0.00 for shipping and tax alongside “Calculated in Checkout.” These are placeholders, not verified free shipping or tax exemption. DigiKey's displayed estimate does not establish final sales tax. We did not supply a shipping address or enter checkout to obtain destination-specific charges.
