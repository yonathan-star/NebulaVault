Day 1: Polish Design Idea and Start CAD

Today I worked on my design idea and CAD.

Basically, my design is something that can turn on the light for me without me getting out of bed. My primary concern was the way to mount it to the wall, because if it stained the wall, my mum would get real mad at me. I decided to use some Command tape (I don't know the official name):

Then, I started working on my BOM. For my project, I knew I wanted some mechanism to lift the light, but I wasn't sure how. I was debating between a pulley system and a physical rack and pinion when I came up with this:

The idea is to have a wheel with spokes that pushes the light switch on and off. For this, you need decent torque, so I ordered the MG90S, a metal-gear servo motor.

Then, I realized that I had run out of wires to use at my home for projects, so I needed to some wire, so I put an order for a roll of solid-core wire.

I alse needed an IR module so I could turn it on and off remotely:

And a power cable for power:

Then I started on my CAD. I did not get much done because V1 was expensive, so I slimmed it down, and I need to start working on the finer aspects of it
