Ok so lots of research and experiments later, I have started my project:

This is what the final product will have: (2 Stepper motors to move arms of robot from a single shared shaft which will contain bearings)

I have used respective color for what each stepper will control

Firstly I wasted some time figuring out the dimensions of stepper and then the GT2 and the bearings but in the end, for the stepper and GT2, I have decided to import CAD models to use as reference

Then I ran into another problem, i.e how would I control two separate part from one point and while they are conflicting each other for control.

The solution, "I just moved a stepper up" (It took for me a full day's break just to realize T_T)

One of the solutions I had tried:

Though it looks identical, the GT2's ends have been flipped and one goes more depth inside of the body of the project. But ultimately, it didn't provide the clearance I had hoped for.

Another one was trying to extend the ends of the Stepper B, and after trying many times I just gave up on creating the symmetry and decided to lift stepper B up:
Now, I can just adjust the height of stepper by increasing matterial beneath (And yes its a box, where you would put in the stepper, though it looks like a solid block from this perspective)

And now currently I am working on the main shaft before moving on to the arm:

The grey one is placeholder for a bearing of: 12mm Inner Diameter x 21mm Outer Diameter x 5mm Height

I am trying to create a shape that can be glued on to the bearing that will be controlled by the stepper and also extends to be the arm control
